It’s finally here! 🤩 The NATS team is thrilled to announce the 2.10 release of the NATS Server! We want to thank everyone who contributed through discussions, raising issues, code contribution and testing early versions. ❤️ The journey to 2.10 Before diving into 2.10 itself, let’s reflect on the journey leading up to this release and why it is significant. It has been just over a year since the 2.9.x series started and a record 22 patch releases have landed since then.
