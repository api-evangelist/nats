We are thrilled to announce that NATS Server 2.11 is finally here! We want to extend a heartfelt thank you to everyone who contributed through discussions, issue reporting, code contributions, and testing early versions. ❤️ A Long Journey to 2.11 The 2.10.x series was our longest yet, having currently reached 26 patch releases. Throughout this extended period, our ongoing commitment to testing and quality assurance delivered excellent results, making 2.10.26 the most stable and reliable release to date.
