Agent systems are becoming distributed systems, and NATS already solves many of the communication problems those systems are rediscovering. NATS has long been a natural fit for distributed, heterogeneous systems. No matter where your devices run, what languages your services use, or how ephemeral your clients are, it gives them a simple way to find and talk to one another.
